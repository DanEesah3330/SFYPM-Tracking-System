Supervisor assignment

2

Topic submission and review

Submit a topic, get approved, rejected or asked to revise, with clear feedback each time.

3

Milestones and deadlines

Supervisors set milestones with deadlines. Students update their progress as they work.

Documents and feedback

Notifications

Student

See your supervisor, submit topics, update milestones, upload documents and read feedback.

Supervisor

Review topics, create milestones, monitor progress and give feedback to your students.

Project Coordinator
